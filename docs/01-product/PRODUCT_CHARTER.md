# Product charter

**Baseline:** 1.0, 2026-09-16. **Status:** architecture proposal grounded in the founder's brief; not market validation.

## Purpose

Build a place where girls and women can record their story, explore what matters to them and find a useful next opportunity without being reduced to an advertising or surveillance profile. Parents can support a child without owning her identity forever. Women who join at 52 or 74 receive a useful product on day one, not an empty childhood timeline.

The core brand is **HerPages — An app that grows with her.** The internal product principle is **Her story belongs to her.** Avoid claiming lifetime availability: portability and a shutdown/export plan support continuity without promising that a company will exist forever.

## The product loop

**Choose an interest or goal → do one small real-world activity → record what mattered → discover a relevant next step → decide what, if anything, to share.**

HerPages should reward agency and usefulness, not time spent scrolling. Journaling is optional; users need not reveal intimate information to receive general educational content or browse opportunities.

## Initial problem hypothesis

Parents may struggle to turn scattered guidance into age-appropriate activities, preserve meaningful milestones and find trustworthy opportunities. Girls may want a voice that is not simply a parent's performance dashboard. Adult women may need continuity across career changes, interests, mentoring and personal goals. These are hypotheses to test through the research plan, not conclusions derived from the size of the female population.

## Positioning

A private journey and growth companion with a curated connected layer. Not a parenting diagnosis tool, parental surveillance product, generic social network, emergency service, medical system or job-placement guarantee.

The distinctive combination is a portable personal history, transparent sharing, life-stage-sensitive experiences and credible opportunities. Existing products already cover journaling, family albums and women's careers separately; the competitive analysis makes no claim that HerPages has invented those categories.

## People and boundaries

The principal users are girls and women. Guardians of any gender may support authorized child profiles. The person who pays does not automatically own the data. Mentors, content editors and partner organizations have narrow roles, not general profile access. Staff cannot gain private-vault keys merely by having an administrator role.

Avoid cultural prescriptions. Do not presume marriage, motherhood, biological children, university study, a salaried career or retirement. A woman can learn coding at 65, choose caregiving at 23, restart work at 48 or focus on art. Age suggests a starting experience; the user chooses her goals and appearance.

## Scope and exclusions

The complete vision includes private Pages, parent support, growth activities, opportunities, age-controlled community, mentoring, safety resources and optional trusted contacts. Implementation is phased by evidence and risk. Open child social networking, automatic police dispatch, continuous child tracking, diagnoses, investment recommendations and automated hiring decisions are excluded from the baseline.

The founder's remote-operating constraint means no business dependency on physical candidate enrollment, field verification or an owned emergency network. Remote operations still require real editorial, moderation, privacy and support work as features expand.

## Success criteria

Success is users returning because a real-world next step was useful and because they understand who can see their information. Establish baselines through consensual research before targets become forecasts. Revenue must sustain storage, support, security and editorial work without selling private data.

Stop or narrow the scope if users value only commodity photo storage, will not return after onboarding, cannot understand recovery/sharing, or if safe moderation is unaffordable. A beautiful multigenerational vision is not itself evidence of retention.

## Decision rights

The owner approves scope, budget and commercial direction. Engineering owns feasibility and implementation evidence. Qualified privacy/legal reviewers approve jurisdiction and child-consent readiness. Security review gates encryption and recovery. Safeguarding review gates minor experiences and mentoring. No unstaffed role may be labeled signed off.
