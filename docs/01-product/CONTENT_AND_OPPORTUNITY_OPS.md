# Content, opportunity and partner operations

The catalog is a product operation, not an LLM-generated list. The first release uses a small manually curated catalog with source evidence. Do not scrape platforms or ingest copyrighted program databases without rights.

## Opportunity record

Required fields: stable ID; title; organizer; organizer-verification status; official source URL; application destination; category; countries; remote/onsite; coarse region where relevant; inclusive eligibility bounds where published; other criteria as factual text; cost/currency or explicit unknown; deadline and timezone or explicit open-ended; publication status; first/last verified dates; reviewer ID; next review date; source-change note; age-suitability assessment.

Do not invent an age rule or fee from context. Model unknown separately from not applicable and false. An explicitly women-focused opportunity can be cataloged without claiming that every applicant qualifies. Show the organizer's current eligibility and let the user decide.

## Editorial workflow

`draft → evidence_pending → under_review → approved → published → recheck_due → suspended/expired → archived`.

A source URL alone does not prove legitimacy. Check the domain, organization identity, application destination, fees, privacy terms and unusual requests. A second reviewer approves high-risk listings, such as an onsite youth event or a program requesting identity documents. No organizer can approve its own listing. Preserve material change history.

Recheck suggested cadences: before publication; at least weekly for open time-sensitive opportunities; within 48 hours of a deadline; immediately when reported. These are proposed operating targets and require staffing. Expiry is automated from the stored deadline; editorial verification is not faked by a cron heartbeat.

## Recommendations

Download a permitted general catalog slice without uploading precise personal preferences. Match explicitly chosen interests and eligibility locally. Reasons are simple: 'You selected science' or 'Remote, open to the chosen age range.' Never infer vulnerability, health, religion, household income or gender from Pages. If an application requires sensitive eligibility information, collect it only in a separately reviewed and explicit application flow, not for discovery.

## Parenting and growth content

Every activity has purpose, suitable age range, estimated time, accessibility adaptations, materials, safety considerations, content source/licensing, reviewer role and review date. Activities are suggestions, not developmental tests. Avoid assuming body size, ability, mobility, academic performance or family structure. Provide non-screen and low-cost alternatives.

Clinical, puberty, abuse-prevention, financial and legal content requires qualified subject review. No crowdsourced medical advice. A model may draft editorial content, but never publish without review. Telugu and later translations require human review for high-stakes meaning, not only fluent machine translation.

## Partner verification and tenancy

Separate organization administrator, contributor and reviewer roles. Verify contact channels and authorization to represent the organization. Store evidence references with restricted access; avoid public uploads of identity documents. A badge describes the check performed and date; it is not a safety certificate.

Partners see their own programs and intentional applications only. They cannot browse non-applicants, infer minors from small counts or export users into advertising systems. Applicant data has a declared purpose, retention and deletion route. Revoked organizational staff immediately lose access, including to queued exports.

## Reporting and correction

Offer 'wrong information', 'expired', 'suspicious fee', 'unsafe request' and 'other' reports. Suspend a potentially harmful listing pending review. Inform affected applicants through approved minimal notifications without revealing private interests. Keep a correction log and a review escalation route.

## Bootstrap requirement

Synthetic sample listings must display 'DEMO — not a real opportunity' and use reserved example domains. Before a real beta, curate and verify a small scoped catalog; the target quantity is an operating choice, not a promise of partnerships. Do not reuse names of real corporations to make a fictional scholarship look authentic.
