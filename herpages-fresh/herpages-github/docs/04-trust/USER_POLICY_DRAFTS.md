# User-facing policy and notice drafts

**Not approved for publication.** These are content structures and sample plain language for counsel, safeguarding and product review. Legal entity, contacts, provider names, retention dates and jurisdiction terms must be supplied from verified operating facts. Do not deploy a generic privacy-policy generator as a substitute.

## Onboarding storage explanation

'Private Pages are stored in an encrypted vault on this device. Without a recoverable backup, losing this device or its keys may mean losing those Pages. You can choose encrypted backup after setting up recovery. HerPages still processes limited service information when you use accounts or connected features. Community posts and information you choose to send to a partner or AI service have different visibility.'

## Optional backup consent

Explain encrypted objects, server-visible metadata, key custody, recovery method, quota, backup history, withdrawal/deletion and provider location. Show a separate optional control. Do not say account-password reset will recover the vault. Require recovery rehearsal before activation.

## Guardian notice

State whose information is processed, verified relationship, each purpose, minimum fields, who can see each collection, child's participation choices, how withdrawal works and the adulthood transition. Explain separate parent-private and child-history collections. No promise of absolute secrecy to a child when a guardian holds the same key.

## AI disclosure

'You selected these items for this request. To answer, the approved AI service will receive this selected text. It is no longer private solely to your device for that processing. Review the provider and retention information before sending.' Show exact selected content, cancel, provider details and alternatives. Minor personal-data requests remain disabled in the baseline.

## Community notice

'Content posted here can be read by the selected community and authorized moderation staff. Keep your private address, school timetable, identity documents and private contact details out of posts. Report or block unwanted behavior. A membership or verification badge does not guarantee that another person is safe.'

## Safety wording

'HerPages helps you access official resources and, where enabled, contact people you choose. It is not an emergency-response service and cannot guarantee message delivery or police arrival. For immediate danger, use the appropriate official emergency service.' The actual in-app copy must use verified country-specific resources and accessible wording.

## Subscription wording

Clearly state price, taxes where applicable, period, automatic renewal, cancellation, storage allowance, grace/export behavior, refunds and purchase restoration. Payment does not grant access to another person's private Pages. Free official help and data export are not removed to pressure renewal.

## Full policy documents required before release

Privacy notice; child/guardian notice; terms of use; community standards; acceptable-use and moderation appeals; subscription/refund terms; partner data-processing terms; security reporting; grievance/contact page; processor list; retention schedule; AI disclosure; emergency limitations. Store them as approved versioned artifacts after review, recording approver/date/scope. This draft file does not imply approval.
