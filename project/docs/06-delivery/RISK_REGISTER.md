# Product and delivery risk register

| ID | Risk | Severity | Mitigation / evidence | Owner role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Broad lifelong vision lacks a useful first week | High | Research concrete tasks; ship private capture plus one useful next step | Product |
| R-02 | Recovery loses irreplaceable memories | Critical | Clean-device restore, key-loss UX, expert crypto review | Security/engineering |
| R-03 | Parents interpret app as surveillance | Critical | Separate collections, clear sharing, child agency and safeguarding review | Product/safeguarding |
| R-04 | Child law/consent misunderstood | Critical | Current phased legal register, assurance provider and launch gate | Privacy/counsel |
| R-05 | False safety assurance | Critical | Directory-first, no dispatch promise, tested failure states | Safety/product |
| R-06 | Community grooming/harassment exceeds capacity | Critical | Adult-first, no minor DMs, staffed moderation, pause capability | Safeguarding |
| R-07 | Modern colors fail accessibility | High | Semantic contrast pairs, real assistive testing, user-controlled themes | Design |
| R-08 | Bolt generates unportable web-only code | High | Native spike first, contracts, clean-room Codex handover | Engineering |
| R-09 | Provider or library changes privacy/security behavior | High | Pinned versions, current sources, adapter review, kill switches | Engineering/privacy |
| R-10 | E2EE claims ignore metadata or AI disclosure | High | Data-class labels, processor inventory, honest notices | Privacy |
| R-11 | Catalog becomes stale or contains scams | High | Source-based editorial workflow, expiry and takedown | Editorial |
| R-12 | Revenue insufficient for media/support/moderation | High | Cost caps, no unlimited promises, paid validation | Owner/product |
| R-13 | Women-only claim cannot be guaranteed | High | Precise eligibility wording, conduct controls, appeals, no gender classifier | Product/safeguarding |
| R-14 | Public repository exposes private data or IP | High | Synthetic fixtures, secret checks, licensing review | Owner/security |
| R-15 | Founder capacity spread across too many products | High | Evidence-based critical path, bounded tranche, no fake completion percentages | Owner |
| R-16 | Older users feel stereotyped or excluded | Medium/high | Separate 50+/65+ research, current-goal onboarding, optional accessibility | Research/design |
| R-17 | Parent/adult transfer dispute has no safe workflow | High | Legal review, independent assurance, stalled/disputed state, no blanket transfer | Privacy/safeguarding |
| R-18 | Paid partner model pressures audience access | High | No non-applicant browsing, aggregation controls, contractual purpose | Product/privacy |

Track mitigation status in task evidence. Severity is a planning judgment, not a statistical risk calculation. None of these risks is closed merely by writing this register.
