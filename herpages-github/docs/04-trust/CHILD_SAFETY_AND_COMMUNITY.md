# Child safety, community and safeguarding operations

## Release posture

HerPages is not an open children's social network. Private local Pages and public/connected content are different systems. Initial releases have no stranger discovery, public minor profile, adult-to-minor direct messages, anonymous random chat or live minor location. A feature flag alone cannot authorize community launch; trained operating capacity and safety/legal gates are required.

## Community eligibility

Girls'/women's community membership is a published policy separate from guardian access. Do not infer gender from faces, voices, clothing, names or identity-document appearance. Do not advertise that every member is guaranteed to be a woman or safe. Use clear conduct rules, proportionate verification where justified, account-integrity controls, human review and a respectful appeal process. Final eligibility and inclusion language requires founder, safeguarding and legal review before launch.

Parents/guardians of any gender can support approved child profiles without gaining entry into restricted communities. Paying for a subscription does not bypass eligibility. Mentors cannot use professional status to bypass age boundaries.

## Adult community first

After the community gate, adults may join approved groups with reporting, blocking, rate limits and visible rules. Moderators review connected content under a disclosed policy. Block/report controls must be reachable from the relevant item. No recommendation engine pushes intimate distress content to maximize engagement. Avoid public follower/beauty/popularity rankings.

Future teen circles require closed age cohorts, reviewed organizers, verified guardian flow where required, trained moderators and controlled interactions. No adult-minor private inbox. Private mentor contact must not be moved off-platform through links or disguised contact details without detection and review appropriate to the program.

## Abuse model

Risks include grooming, sexual harassment, impersonation, coercive family monitoring, sextortion, image abuse, bullying, self-harm encouragement, fraudulent opportunities, doxxing and predatory recruitment. Reporting must work without requiring a public reply or confronting the suspected abuser. Never ask someone to upload more abusive imagery as proof when a content reference is sufficient.

Private encrypted vaults are not proactively read by moderators. A user can intentionally disclose selected material for help, with a clear preview. No promise that the service detects all abuse inside private Pages.

## Moderation workflow

`submitted → triaged → assigned → investigating → actioned → appeal_window → closed`, with emergency/legal escalation and evidence-preservation side states.

Each case records exact content reference, reporter access limits, category, urgency, assigned reviewer, actions, rationale and appeal. Reviewers see only necessary case data. Report visibility must not reveal the reporter to the reported person. Automated classifiers prioritize but do not make every final safeguarding decision.

Define coverage and capacity before enablement. Proposed triage targets are operating commitments to approve, not current guarantees. Do not show '24/7 monitored' without staffed coverage. If coverage is unavailable, pause risky publishing/mentoring features and keep official help resources accessible.

## Escalation

Create jurisdiction-specific procedures for imminent danger, child sexual abuse material, credible threats and mandatory reporting. Qualified counsel/safeguarding specialists must establish lawful authorities, evidence handling and timelines. Do not invent a global reporting duty or let an LLM decide whether a statutory report is required. Do not automatically notify a guardian when that guardian may be implicated.

Workers handling sensitive reports need training, access controls, wellbeing support and rotation. Store evidence in a restricted system, never GitHub, general analytics or public issue screenshots. Retention must reflect safety, appeal and legal needs without indefinite hoarding.

## Community standards

Require respectful conduct; prohibit sexual exploitation, harassment, threats, non-consensual personal information, impersonation, scams and attempts to move minors into private adult contact. Clearly distinguish disagreement from abuse and provide reasons/appeals. No facial attractiveness filters or body-shaming gamification.

## Events and mentors

Verification badges state what was checked and when, not blanket safety approval. Onsite youth events need separate organization, safeguarding and consent checks. A listed event is not an emergency-response guarantee. Mentor programs define scope, session boundaries, reporting, supervision and termination. Background checks are not assumed to be available through software alone.

## Prelaunch evidence

Test grooming attempts, age mixing, blocked-user evasion, malicious uploads, unsafe external links, report confidentiality, moderator abuse, appeals, notification leakage, compromised partner accounts and capacity overload. App-store UGC/child-safety requirements must be checked against current official rules. [S09, S21, S22]
